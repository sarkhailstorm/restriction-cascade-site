Support

Email support@findigo.in. We aim to reply within two business days (India time).

Before you write

The troubleshooting section covers the most common questions, including a dialog that never opens.

What to include

  • Your Confluence site address, for example your-company.atlassian.net.
  • The space key and the title of the root page, if your question is about a cascade.
  • What you expected, what happened instead, and roughly when.
  • A screenshot of the Restriction Cascade dialog or the activity log entry, if you have one.

Please don't send page content or passwords. We never need them.

Security issues

Report suspected security issues to support@findigo.in with "Security" in the subject. We treat them as a priority.
